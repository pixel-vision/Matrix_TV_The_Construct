# Privacy Policy — The Construct

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

The Construct is a free, open-source home screen for Android / Google TV. It is a personal hobby project with no company, no servers, no ads and no analytics.

## What it uses from Trakt

If you choose to link your Trakt account, The Construct reads:

- the shows you watch and your watch progress
- your watchlist
- your episode calendar (upcoming and newly aired episodes)

It uses this only to show you a notice on your TV when a new episode of a show you watch has aired.

The Construct is **read only**. It never adds to, changes or deletes anything on your Trakt account.

## Where your data goes

Nowhere. Your Trakt sign-in token and anything read from Trakt stay on your own TV and home network. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else, and nothing is sold or shared.

## Unlinking

You can unlink at any time from your Trakt account settings (Connected Apps) or by removing the Trakt settings from The Construct. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.
